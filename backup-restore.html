Backup (Export)

Yahan se tables ki nakal CSV file mein download ho jati hai (Excel mein khul jati hai). Ye aap ka apna alag mehfooz record hai.

Dhyan rakhein: in files mein users ka naam, email aur phone hota hai. Inhein kisi ke saath share na karein aur mehfooz jagah rakhein. KYC ke CNIC aur tasveeren is mein shamil nahi hain. Wapas bahal karna (restore) yahan se nahi hota. Asli bachao ke liye Supabase mein Database, phir Backups dekhein.
