KYC Settings

General Verification

Configure the basic KYC requirements.

KYC Verification Enable or disable the KYC requirement.
Require KYC for Withdrawals Mark verification as required before withdrawal requests.
Require KYC for Account Activation Indicate that verification is required for activation.
Allow Resubmission Allow users to resubmit rejected documents.
